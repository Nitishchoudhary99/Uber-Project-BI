_Prepared 26 Oct 2026. Every claim carries a confidence tag. **High** = official/primary source. **Medium** = credible reporting, single source or not independently checked. **Low** = vendor blog or marketing, treat as a hypothesis._
_Where sources disagree, both numbers are shown. Nothing here is a substitute for primary research with real job seekers (see section 7)._

## 2. What this means (market issues, ranked by threat to the idea)

1. **Platform dependence is the existential risk.** The jobs live on Naukri/LinkedIn/foundit. We cannot scrape or log in on the user's behalf (M7, M8). Our access to those portals is the user's own alert email and the user's own browser. If a portal changes alert formats or blocks forwarding, our supply of those jobs shrinks overnight. Mitigation: diversify supply (company career pages, JSON-LD, more ATS types), and pursue partnerships early.
2. **Incumbents are adding AI** (M6). A generic "AI job search" is not a moat. What incumbents structurally *won't* do: aggregate across competing portals, tell the user a posting is a probable scam on a rival's site, or keep the user's data portable.
3. **Trust is the unmet need, not just speed** (M4). 93% meet scams and half cannot tell. A visible trust verdict on every listing could matter more than a better match score, and it also protects our own users.
4. **Willingness to pay is unproven** (M10). Price anchors are about ₹700-890/month for a service people doubt. We must test, not assume. Fresher segment: biggest volume, most scam-exposed, least able to pay.
5. **Compliance clock is running** (M11, M12). DPDP obligations land within 6-18 months; Google verification takes weeks and recurring cost. Both can block launch if started late.
6. **Demand-side uncertainty** (M3). If hiring is flat or falling in IT services/BFSI, users apply to more jobs for fewer responses: good for a tool that saves time, bad for assumptions about conversion. We should not promise "more interviews".
7. **Supply quality from alert emails is thin** (title, company, city only). Matching on title alone is weak. We need a "fetch the full listing" step that respects each site's terms (user-driven opening of the link; extension reads the page the user is viewing).

## 3. Where the current build stands against these issues

| Issue | Current state | Gap |
|---|---|---|
| Platform dependence | Email alerts + public ATS + JSON-LD; no logins, no scraping | ATS list skews global (Greenhouse/Lever/Ashby). No Zoho Recruit, Darwinbox, Workday, SmartRecruiters, Keka connectors. Live connectors untested |
| Trust/scams | Only stale and thin-description flags | **No scam/trust scoring at all** |
| AI table stakes | Rule-based matching and CV selection | No natural-language search, no LLM rephrasing (deliberately), no interview prep |
| Mobile-first India | Responsive web only | No installable PWA, no push/WhatsApp, English only |
| Gmail verification | Works for test users; CASA not started | Need an alternative path that avoids the restricted scope (forwarding inbox, section 4 of the design) |
| DPDP | Consent, audit, export, delete | No notices in Indian languages, no minor/guardian flow, no breach process, no data-retention policy |
| Willingness to pay | None | No pricing experiment, no payments |
| Ghosting | Application tracker + follow-up date | No employer-responsiveness signal |
| Security hygiene | Hashed passwords, token expiry, SSRF guard | No login rate limit, email verification, password reset |

## 4. Recommended positioning and beachhead (hypotheses to validate)

- **Positioning:** "Your job-search copilot: every portal in one list, scam-checked, a truthful tailored CV for each, and applying in a few taps - without risking your Naukri/LinkedIn account."
- **Beachhead:** professionals with **1-6 years of experience in IT/data/digital roles in the top metros.** They already receive alerts from several portals, have the highest pay for time saved, are on laptops *and* phones, and are less scam-vulnerable than freshers. Freshers come second, as a free tier fed by the trust layer (they need it most but pay least).
- **Monetisation hypotheses (to test, not assume):** free core (aggregate, match, trust verdict, 5 tailored CVs/month); paid for unlimited tailoring, automated follow-ups, deeper insights, interview prep. Test price points below the ~₹700-890 anchor, e.g. ₹199-399/month, with a 1-month pass for active searchers (searching is episodic: people churn once hired).
- **Do not promise** "more interviews" or "apply to 1,000 jobs". Promise time saved, fewer scams, better-fit applications.

## 5. Open facts to establish before committing money
1. Which ATS/career platforms do the top 100 employers in the beachhead actually use? (sample 100 career pages by hand)
2. What share of a typical user's job leads arrive as alert emails, and from which portals? (10 user interviews + inbox sampling with consent)
3. Real willingness to pay and price sensitivity (landing-page test, 5-minute survey, pre-sale).
4. Official CASA cost/timeline from a Google-approved lab (get 2 quotes).
5. DPDP applicability details (we are a data fiduciary; are job seekers' CV data "significant"? children?) with a lawyer.
